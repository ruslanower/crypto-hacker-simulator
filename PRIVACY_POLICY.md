# Privacy Policy for Crypto Hacker Simulator

**Last Updated:** October 30, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how "Crypto Hacker Simulator" ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information. The App is developed as a simulated cyber clicker and strategy game designed strictly for entertainment purposes.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
**Crypto Hacker Simulator does NOT collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.**
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, device IDs, or IP addresses.
- **No Financial Data:** The game simulates cryptocurrency transactions, mining, and exchange markets. No real cryptocurrency, credit card, bank account, or digital wallet is ever connected, collected, or accessed. All currency values ($ and BTC/ETH) are entirely fictitious in-game tokens.
- **No Tracking or Third-Party Analytics:** We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID), or behavioural tracking tools.

## 3. Local Storage of Game Data
All game progress (including tap level, owned hardware, botnet state, simulated balances, and unlocked achievements) is stored **locally on your device** using local storage / standard app preferences (`SharedPreferences` / `localStorage`).
- This data remains strictly on your device.
- We do not have access to your saved games.
- You can permanently reset or erase all game progress at any time directly in the game settings ("Darknet -> Factory Reset") or by clearing the app data in your Android system settings (`Settings -> Apps -> Crypto Hacker Simulator -> Storage -> Clear Data`).

## 4. Permissions
The App does not request access to:
- Camera or microphone
- Location services (GPS)
- Contacts, SMS, or Phone calls
- Device storage / photos / media files

## 5. Children's Privacy
Because Crypto Hacker Simulator does not collect any personal data, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The game contains no real gambling or age-inappropriate content.

## 6. Disclaimer Regarding In-Game Currency & Hacking Themes
- All game mechanics depicting "hacking", "heists", "darknet", "crypto trading", and "laundering" are purely fictional, satirical video game mechanics.
- In-game currencies cannot be exchanged, withdrawn, or redeemed for real money, goods, services, or cryptocurrency.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If we make any changes to our privacy practices, this document will be updated accordingly.
